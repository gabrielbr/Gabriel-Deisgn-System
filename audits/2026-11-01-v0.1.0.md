## Scope

Nothing in the apps changed. 0.1.0 captures what both apps ship today:

- `tokens/tokens.json`: `core` (10 colours, 9 spacing, 4 radii, frame, hairline, 2 durations, type
  family) and two layers, `uikit` (NotTheOmiAIApp `ui-kit`) and `pulseloop` (PulseLoopAndroid).
- Generators, checks and a drift report; rules (AGENTS.md), DESIGN.md, docs, fonts, marks, icons.

The audit covers the system as captured plus the kit code that draws it (`Ui.java`), because a
token is only as good as the place it is used.

### Findings

- **[P1] A-2 Snackbar action invisible in dark theme.** `NotTheOmiAIApp/ui-kit/.../Ui.java`
  `snackbar()`: ground `INK` resolves to `#F2F2F2` in dark, action is always `MINT` → **1.27:1**.
  Undo is effectively hidden. Fix: action colour `on-mint`-family dark ink on light ground, e.g.
  a new `snackbar-action` token (`mint` light, `#0A7A55` in dark, 4.8:1 on `#F2F2F2`), or keep the
  snackbar ground dark in both themes. Suggested: `/impeccable colorize`. Tracked in `contrast`.
- **[P1] A-1 Stop label under 4.5:1 in light.** `Ui.java:995`: white on `coral` `#E9554D` =
  **3.58:1** at 16sp bold (WCAG "large" starts at 18.66px bold). Dark already switches to ink.
  Fix: darken light `coral` (`#C8372F` gives 5.2:1) or use ink on coral in both themes; also
  fix the token's usage text, which states the rule wrongly. Tracked in `contrast`.
- **[P1] A-3 Tab badge.** `Ui.java:494–499`: 11sp white on coral (3.58:1 light, 2.54:1 dark),
  under the 12sp floor and against the kit's own spec (badge = coral-text on coral-soft). Fix:
  follow the spec; 12sp.
- **[P2] A-5 Type roles collide.** `label` is 16 bold in uikit and 12 regular in PulseLoop;
  `title` is 22 vs 16. The same word means different things across apps. See D-1.
- **[P2] A-4 Literals inside the kit.** 31 `dp(c, n)` and `Color.WHITE` uses in `Ui.java`
  (badge 18/5/9, STOP 56 vs `button` 52, tab icon 56×32, snackbar elevation 2). `ui_lint.py`
  only scans screens. Fix: tokens for each, then extend the lint to `ui-kit/`.
- **[P2] A-6 Doc drift in NotTheOmiAIApp.** `docs/redesign/design-system/README.md` and
  `IMPLEMENTATION.md` say display 34/40 and body 16/26; tokens and code say 30/36 and 16/24. See D-8.
- **[P2] A-7 Shared data hues.** See D-9.
- **[P3] A-8 PulseLoop body 14sp.** Fundamentals put screen body at 16–18. Defensible for dense
  health cards; revisit with D-1.
- **[P3] A-9 750ms screen wipe.** Long against the 150–300ms guidance; it's the brand's signature,
  so it is a decision (D-2), not a defect.

### Positive findings

- Both apps already match the system exactly: `drift.py` reports **0 differences**.
- Every core text pair passes AA in every shipped theme; `muted` 5.8:1 light / 7.8:1 dark.
- One accent with a dedicated on-colour (9.7:1); success split from the brand in PulseLoop.
- Reduce-motion honoured in both kits; 48dp targets; switch rows are one control.
