Findings

- **[P1] A-10 (new, pre-existing in the app) Dark values collapse by light value.**
  `Ui.color(c, X)` → `UiTokens.dark(X)` searches `LIGHT` for X's value and returns the first match's
  dark value. `PANEL` (= `INK` in light) drew `INK_DARK` `#F2F2F2` instead of `#000000`, and
  `ON_PANEL` (= `SURFACE`) drew `#1C1B1F`. Visible in `docs/ui/redesign/gvoice-detail-dark.png`:
  a light player in a dark screen, its times at about 2.1:1. Fix here: the `pair` mark, its check and
  the generated comment. Fix in the app: read those four as pairs. Suggested: `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P1] A-11 (new, pre-existing in the app) Active tab icon on mint in dark.** The tab bar tints
  the active icon `color(c, INK)`, which is `#F2F2F2` in dark: near-white on the mint pill, 1.4:1
  (WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1). Breaks the system's own rule (text and icons on mint are ink or
  on-mint). No token change needed; fixed in the NotTheOmiAIApp sync PR (raw `INK` in both
  themes, as the PRIMARY button already does), checked in the rendered dark screenshots.
- **[P3] P-1** as above.

The 0.1.0 items A-1, A-2, A-3 (and A-11) are closed in the system by this change and close in the app with the
NotTheOmiAIApp sync PR. A-4 to A-9 stay open.
