| 5 | Adaptivity | 3 | Unchanged |

Critique: the variant earns its place. It keeps the product's restraint (Operate mode) and puts
the delight in one moment, the reveal, instead of decorating every screen. The line is personal
and short. The brief (PRODUCT.md) is honoured: the frame, the mono type and the one-accent rule all
survive the swap.

Findings

- **[P3] F-1 Pink near coral.** In fairy mode the accent and the alert colour are closer in hue.
  Alerts keep their words and coral-soft ground; the doc says never to put them side by side
  unlabelled.
- **[P3] F-2 Pink near two PulseLoop metric colours** (`metric-blood-pressure`, `metric-drinks`).
  Folded into D-9.
- **[P3] F-3 The celebration swallows taps for up to 4.2s** (1.4s reduced). Acceptable for a
  one-time moment; if it ever plays at a bad time, a tap could end it early.
- **[P3] F-4 The celebration's line sits on the glow's lightest point** (`#FFD6EC` heart) at the
  flash. On the device it reads because the glow fades as the text rises, but it is not
  measurable from tokens: check it on her phone the next time it plays.
