# Audit: Cochilo softer, feminine and maternal (0.11.0)

Copied from the Cochilo repo, `docs/design-review.md`.


Method: design review only (detector not applicable, native platform). Run with the owner's
skills **graphic-design-fundamentals** (§13 critique checklist) and **impeccable** (critique,
Android platform guidance, craft floor), on every Paparazzi screen in light and dark, English and
Portuguese, and the owner's phone screenshot (History, dark, pt-BR). Mode: **Operate** (an app a
tired parent uses one-handed, often at night), so ambition shows as warmth and precision, not
spectacle.

**Brief.** Cochilo is a Tatá Slings product for mothers. It should feel **feminine and maternal**:
warm, soft, calm, reassuring, never clinical or technical. The owner's request: remove the colour
behind titles, and make sure the whole design fits.

## Design specificity verdict

Before this pass the app was half Tatá, half developer tool. The fabric colours, the icon rounds,
the Amora Log button and the fabric frame are specific to this product: nobody else could ship
them. But four inherited system signatures pulled it back toward a generic, technical look:

1. **The teal highlighter behind every screen title** (a solid block under "Histórico"): loud,
   sticker-like, the strongest "tech product" cue on the screen. *The owner flagged it.*
2. **A 4dp teal rule round the whole screen**: in dark it reads as a neon border, a hard edge
   where a mother's app should feel held.
3. **ALL-CAPS tracked captions** ("HOJE", "AWAKE FOR", "RHYTHM, LAST 14 DAYS"): a dashboard's
   voice; and the panel's "AWAKE FOR" is an eyebrow above a number, which impeccable bans.
4. **Teal everywhere**: title, frame, active tab, picked chip, and the main button. Five teal
   things on one screen means teal no longer marks the one action (the 60/30/10 rule broken).

Plus square-shouldered buttons (8dp corners) against the pill-shaped Log menu, and dark-theme
icon rounds so deep they looked muddy on the phone.

## Emotional journey

The peak moment is 3 a.m.: the baby wakes, the phone comes out in the dark. Before: a black
screen with a teal border and teal shouting title. After: a plum-dark screen with a soft hem, a
quiet title, the one teal button and the fabric round the card. The end of the moment (Woke up →
the card fades back to day) was already gentle; now the frame round it is too.

## Priority issues and what was done (0.12.0)

| Severity | Issue (principle) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Teal highlighter behind titles (contrast used as decoration; the owner's request) | Removed. Titles are the display role in ink, semibold (600), plain. |
| Critical | Teal on five things per screen (accent proportion, hierarchy) | Teal is only the one action. New roles: `selected` (a Rosa Chá/Amora blush) for the active tab and a picked chip; `frame-line` (a soft Rosa Chá hem) for the frame. |
| Important | ALL-CAPS tracked captions and the panel eyebrow (voice; impeccable's eyebrow ban) | The caption role is sentence case, 13/18 semibold, no tracking. "Awake for", "Next nap", "Running", "Log". |
| Important | Square buttons beside pill menus (consistency; shape language) | Every button is a pill, like the Log menu, the chips and the widget. Fields stay softly rounded. |
| Important | Muddy dark icon rounds on the phone (value structure in dark) | Every `event-*-soft` dark tint lifted a step; line-on-round contrast still ≥ 3:1. |
| Polish | Display weight 700 felt stern at 30sp (typographic voice) | Display role 600 (Libre Franklin SemiBold). Timer stays bold: it's the number she reads. |
| Polish | "16:13–16:13 · 0min" (match the real world) | Under a minute reads "<1min" / "<1m". |

## Strengths kept

- Her fabrics as data colours and the fabric frame: the most specific, most maternal choices.
- Sleep leads; the Last mark; the motion: calm, short, never decorative.
- One-handed reach: the Log button and the widget.

## Persona red flags

- **Thaíssa at 3 a.m., one hand, baby on the other arm:** the teal frame and title were the
  brightest things in a dark room. Now the brightest thing is the one button she needs.
- **A first-time mother, anxious:** caps and technical labels read as a monitor; sentence case
  reads as a note from someone who cares. (The app still never implies it monitors the baby.)

## Not changed, on purpose

- **Teal itself** stays: it's Tatá Slings' brand colour (logo, site, slings), and the mark.
- **Libre Franklin** stays: the brand's one family (the website). Warmth comes from weight,
  case and shape, not a second typeface.
- **Data colours** stay her fabrics; the actogram's density is data, not decoration.

Questions skipped: the owner asked for the fixes to be applied, not proposed.
