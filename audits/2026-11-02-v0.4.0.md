Critique: the flow is the right size for an Operate surface. It appears at the one moment the person
is already looking at the app, says what changed, and gets out of the way on Later. The reference
implementation's weak spots are taken out of the standard rather than copied:

- **[P1] U-2 No checksum in PulseLoop.** A tampered or truncated-but-complete file would reach
  Android's installer, which only checks the signature. Fixed by the standard (required SHA-256) and
  the PulseLoop PR (publish and verify).
- **[P2] U-1 PulseLoop's update texts skip `tr()`** ("Update available — …", "Downloading…",
  "Check for updates"…), against that repo's own rule. Fixed in the PulseLoop PR with the words above.
- **[P2] U-4 No way out of a long download.** PulseLoop disables both buttons while downloading. For
  GMind's large APK that's minutes with no exit. The standard adds Cancel.
- **[P3] U-3 Separator.** PulseLoop's title uses an em dash; the standard uses "·" like the rest of
  the system's status lines.
