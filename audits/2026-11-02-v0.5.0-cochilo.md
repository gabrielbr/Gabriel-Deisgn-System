**Platform conformance verdict:** passes. It reads as a native Android app themed by the family

pushed screen), bottom sheets for entry and confirmation, snackbar with Undo for deletion.

### Findings

- **[P2] C-1 Night and nap hues are close** (`event-night`/`event-nap` 1.44:1 light, 1.12:1 dark).
  Location: timeline, actogram. Impact: someone who can't tell indigo from blue relies on position
  (night sleep sits at the ends of the bar) and the labelled list. Guideline: WCAG 1.4.1 (not colour
  alone) is met through labels; distinguishing the two at a glance is nicer. Recommendation: keep,
  or give naps a hatch/dither in a later version. `/impeccable colorize`.
- **[P2] C-2 Night viewing (D-11).** The 4dp mint frame and the mint button stay bright at 3am.
  Research favours dim, warm or red light at night. Changing the frame touches a brand commitment, so
  it is recorded as D-11 for the owner, not changed here.
- **[P2] C-4 History is not virtualised.** `ui/screens/HistoryPatterns.kt`: up to 60 days of
  timelines and rows in a `verticalScroll` Column. Fine for weeks, heavy after months.
  Recommendation: `LazyColumn` with day items. `/impeccable optimize`.
- **[P2] C-5 No tablet layout.** Recommendation: navigation rail and 720dp content cap from 600dp,
  as PulseLoop does. `/impeccable adapt`.
- **[P3] C-3 Window background duplicates two tokens.** `res/values*/colors.xml` holds `page` and
  `panel` as hex for the pre-Compose window and launcher. Recommendation: have `gen_tokens.py` also
  emit a `colors.xml` for this layer.
- **[P3] C-6 Remove animations is read once** at composition start, so changing it needs an app
  restart. `/impeccable animate`.
- **[P3] C-7 No device pass yet** (TalkBack, 200% font, gestures). Do it on the Pixel before 1.0.
- **[P3] C-8 Plus icon on every log button** repeats; consider dropping it once the buttons are known.

### Positive findings

- The app copies `generated/cochilo/CTokens.kt` byte for byte: `drift.py --cochilo` reports 0.
- `snackbar-action` passes in both themes (12.6:1 / 4.8:1); the app's snackbar uses it from day one.
- The sleep panel uses the existing `panel` colours: no new pair, and the screen gets darker, not
  brighter, while the baby sleeps.
- Data hues are used only on data and always with a word; every chart has a spoken summary.
