# Audit: wide screens (0.21.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · PR: Cochilo 0.25.0 · Auditor: Claude (graphic-design-fundamentals + impeccable)

## Scope

Cochilo `size` gains `rail` (80), `content-max` (600), `width-medium` (600) and `width-expanded`
(840), Android's window classes. components.md: the Navigation rail row, and Cochilo's two panes
when expanded. No colour changes; contrast unchanged.
