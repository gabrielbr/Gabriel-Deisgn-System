Date: 2026-11-03 · PR: Cochilo 0.26.0 · Auditor: Claude (graphic-design-fundamentals + impeccable)

## Scope

The eleven Tatá Slings texture tiles, remade as true repeating tiles; drawn with a repeat, not
a mirror. No tokens, no rules change ("No text on a texture" still holds).
