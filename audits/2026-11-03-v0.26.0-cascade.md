## Asleep and awake (the timeline pattern in the same release)

- **Colour:** awake has no data colour. It is a hollow ring outlined in `muted`, so event colours stay
  data, and teal stays the one action. Passes in light, dark and night: the ring sits on `surface`,
  and `muted` already passes there as meta text.
- **Hierarchy:** sleep keeps leading (label role, `kind-badge-lead`); awake is body role. Entries
  inside a stretch are set in by `space6`, a step on the spacing scale, never a literal.
- **Reading order:** the time column shows only the stretches' starts, so it runs in order; nested
  entries keep their time in their meta. TalkBack reads each awake stretch as a heading.
- **Targets:** awake rows are not buttons; nested rows keep their 48dp rows and their tap to edit.
