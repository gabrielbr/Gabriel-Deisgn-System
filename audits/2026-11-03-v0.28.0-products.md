Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude · Template: audits/TEMPLATE.md

## Change

The owner's decision: this repo is his boilerplate, in Gabriel's brand, and a partner's brand
(Tatá Slings) and a product's own pieces (Cochilo's layer) live in the product's repo.

This release:
- brings in the generic parts of 0.5.0–0.27.0, made from Cochilo and never merged until now;
- removes the Cochilo layer, the Tatá brand and their assets and generated files;
- turns `cochilo_kt` into a generic `compose_kt`;
- adds `docs/products.md`.
